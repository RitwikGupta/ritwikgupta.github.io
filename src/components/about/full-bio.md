My name is Ritwik Gupta and I am a Ph.D. student at the University of California, Berkeley, where I am a member of the <a href="https://bair.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank">Berkeley AI Research (BAIR)</a> lab. My research focus is on computer vision for humanitarian assistance and disaster response, as well as the development of policies related to the use of machine learning in developing areas. I am co-advised by Drs. <a href="https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~trevor/" target="_blank">Trevor Darrell</a> and <a href="https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Faculty/Homepages/sastry.html" target="_blank">Shankar Sastry</a>. I am an AI Policy Fellow in the <a href="https://csp.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank">Center for Security in Politics</a> under the advisement of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Napolitano" target="_blank">Sec. Janet Napolitano</a>.

I am a Graduate Fellow with the <a href="https://brsl.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank">Berkeley Risk and Security Lab</a>, a Research Fellow at Berkeley’s <a href="https://humanrights.berkeley.edu/home" target="_blank">Human Rights Center</a>, and a Fellow at Berkeley’s <a href="https://cltc.berkeley.edu/program/ai-policy-hub/" target="_blank">AI Policy Hub</a>.

In addition to my academic pursuits, I am the Founder and President of <a href="https://neuraltangent.com/" target="_blank">Neural Tangent</a>, a company that uses machine learning to solve problems in the field of humanitarian assistance and disaster response. I also provide consulting services in the areas of machine learning, artificial intelligence, edge computing, and remote sensing.

I co-organize the <a href="https://ai-climate.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank">Berkeley AI Reseach Climate Initiative</a> with <a href="https://medhini.github.io/" target="_blank">Medhini Narasimhan</a>, which brings together researchers to work on AI research through the lens of climate change and helps to translate that work into real-world applications.
