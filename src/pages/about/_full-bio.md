My name is Ritwik Gupta and I am a Ph.D. student at the University of California, Berkeley, where I am a member of the <a href="https://bair.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank">Berkeley AI Research (BAIR)</a> and the <a href="https://brsl.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank">Berkeley Risk and Security Lab</a> (BRSL) labs. My research focus is on computer vision for chaotic environments, as well as the governance of dual-use AI in both civil and defense contexts. I am co-advised by Drs. <a href="https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~trevor/" target="_blank">Trevor Darrell</a> and <a href="https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Faculty/Homepages/sastry.html" target="_blank">Shankar Sastry</a>. I am an AI Policy Fellow in the <a href="https://csp.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank">Center for Security in Politics</a> under the advisement of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Napolitano" target="_blank">Sec.
